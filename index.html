Consumer Choice Study

Compare Marketplace Offers & Choose a Seller

In this study, you will review four everyday online shopping tasks. In each task, ten different sellers offer the exact same product. Please evaluate the available offers as you would in a real online store and select one seller to purchase from.

Estimated time: 5–7 minutes 4 shopping tasks · 10 offers each US English · USD pricing

What to Expect

  • Identical Product: Within each task, all ten offers are for the exact same item.
  • Seller Information: Each listing displays the seller's name, star rating, customer rating count, and time active on the marketplace.
  • Detailed Views: You can click to view full offer details or any seller's profile before deciding.
  • No Real Purchases: No payment details, shipping address, or email are requested.
