Consumer Choice Study

Compare Marketplace Offers & Choose a Seller

In this study, you will review four everyday online shopping tasks. In each task, six different sellers offer the exact same product at the same price. Please evaluate the offers as you would in a real online store and select one seller to purchase from.

Estimated time: 5–7 minutes 4 shopping tasks · 6 offers each USD pricing · No real payment

What to Expect

  • Identical Products: Within each task, all six offers are for the exact same item.
  • Seller Information: Each listing shows the seller's name, rating, number of ratings, tenure, and store catalog size.
  • Detailed Views: You can click to view full product specifications or any seller's profile before deciding.
  • No Real Purchases: No payment details, shipping address, or email are requested.
